## Research

I lead the [Computational Multiphase Physics (CoMPhy) Lab](https://comphy-lab.org) at Durham. We study **non-Newtonian free-surface flows** and **soft-matter singularities**—drops, bubbles, jets, and sheets where interfaces change rapidly.

Work combines high-fidelity simulation (Basilisk DNS), theory, and collaborative experiments, linking microstructural stress to continuum dynamics and applications in industry and nature. See [research](https://comphy-lab.org/research).
